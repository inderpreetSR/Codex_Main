Customer churn · Learn

Understand the question.

A first look at predicting which customers may leave a service.

Which customers might leave?

Imagine a subscription service wants to understand which active customers may cancel within the next 30 days.

What could a prediction help with?

A team could investigate why customers leave and consider support options. A prediction alone does not explain why.

Key idea · Prediction time

Use only information available before cancellation. Otherwise, the model may learn from the future.
